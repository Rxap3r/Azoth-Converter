Smaller videos for e-mail and messengers, better AI enlarging, easier bank statements in Excel, and clearer messages.

## What's new

- **Make videos smaller.** A new **Make smaller** choice for videos saves a much smaller MP4 copy, called "name (smaller).mp4", for e-mail and messengers: **Full HD**, **HD** (the usual choice) or **Small**. iPhone HDR videos get normal colours, upright videos stay upright, and videos with 60 frames per second get 30. Small videos are never made bigger, and if the copy would not be smaller, nothing is saved. Also in Explorer's right-click menu: right-click a video → **Convert with Azoth Converter → Make smaller**.
- **"Best quality (slow)" for Enhance with AI.** A larger AI model gives more natural hair, fur and grass when small pictures are made much bigger. It is about 15 times slower (some minutes for each picture), so it is off unless you tick it.
- **PDF → Excel: "Tables only" and "All pages on one sheet".** Under **More options** when a PDF becomes Excel. With both ticked, a table that continues over several pages, like a bank statement, becomes one table with its header once, ready to sort and filter. The app remembers your choice.
- **Clearer messages.** Error messages, hover texts and the command-line help now use Simplified Technical English: short sentences, clear instructions, and they say plainly when no file was saved.
- **Faster.** Adding many large PNG pictures no longer makes the window hang (100 large PNGs: from about 20 seconds to under one second), long file lists fill faster, and page previews in the PDF tools no longer make the window stutter.

## Known limitations

- **Best quality (slow)** takes some minutes for each picture on a usual computer. The download is about 30 MB bigger because of its AI model.
- **Make smaller** always saves MP4 and changes the picture a little (it is lossy on purpose). Very short or already small videos can stay the same size; then nothing is saved.
- PDF → Excel finds tables by their visible lines; tables without lines and scanned PDFs aren't supported yet (convert scans to Word instead).
- The right-click menu appears under **Show more options** on Windows 11; the short Windows 11 menu needs a signed app, which comes with code signing.
- Text recognition takes a few seconds per page. Check names and numbers in the result: unclear print or handwriting can be misread.
