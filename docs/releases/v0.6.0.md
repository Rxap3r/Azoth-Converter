PDF → Excel, video and audio conversion (with metadata removal), and a calmer, tidier app.

## What's new

- **PDF → Excel.** Tables in a PDF become real Excel cells, one sheet per page, with merged cells, bold text, colours and column widths. Amounts become numbers you can calculate with only where their meaning is clear; codes like "007" and account numbers stay text. Text outside the tables is kept too. Shows progress and can be cancelled.
- **Video and audio.** Convert videos (MP4, MOV from iPhones, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, MPG, MTS, FLV, 3GP) to MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI or GIF, or save just the sound as MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG or Opus. Music converts between MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus (also from WMA, AIFF, AAC). Animated GIFs become much smaller MP4 or WebM videos. Wherever possible picture and sound are copied unchanged, so nothing is lost and it takes seconds. Shows progress and can be cancelled. Everything needed is included in the app.
- **Remove metadata from videos and music.** **Remove metadata only** and **Show metadata…** now also work for videos and music: the place a video was filmed (GPS), the phone model, dates and software are removed, while picture and sound stay bit for bit the same. Song titles, artists and covers are kept. Converted videos lose this information automatically, like photos.
- **A calmer app.** The Convert page shows the 6 most useful formats (the rest under **More formats**); rarely changed choices are under **More options**; explanations appear when you hover over a choice. Settings and the sidebar are tidier.
- **Right-click menu uses the open window.** Files chosen in Explorer now go to the Azoth Converter window that is already open instead of starting a second copy.
- **Fixed:** small empty windows no longer flash up while the app starts. Double-clicking `azoth-terminal.exe` now opens a window where you can type commands (before, Enter just closed it), and wildcards such as `*.heic` work in Windows terminals.

## Known limitations

- PDF → Excel finds tables by their visible lines; tables without lines and scanned PDFs aren't supported yet (convert scans to Word instead).
- Videos that need re-encoding (e.g. to WebM or GIF) can take a while; long videos to GIF make very large files.
- The download is about 75 MB bigger because the video tools are included.
- The right-click menu appears under **Show more options** on Windows 11; the short Windows 11 menu needs a signed app, which comes with code signing.
- Text recognition takes a few seconds per page. Check names and numbers in the result: unclear print or handwriting can be misread.
